Meet your instructor

Meet Dr. Tejas Patel

Dr. Patel is one of the top cosmetic dentists in the US, with over 20 years of experience placing porcelain veneers and thousands of smile makeovers completed in his fee-for-service cosmetic practice in Austin, Texas, where over half of patients fly in from other cities and states. Along the way he built one of the largest social media audiences in cosmetic dentistry, growing past 2.6 million followers.

“I’m teaching because this is something I wish was available to me when I was a new dentist. For me to learn cosmetic dentistry, it took tons of research, years of courses, and thousands of dollars. We have a tendency as dentists to overcomplicate things. My goal in teaching is to take complex things and simplify them.”
